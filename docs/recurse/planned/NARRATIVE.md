# Narrative and Environmental Storytelling

> **Not yet implemented.** Design placeholder for a confirmed game feature.

## Purpose

Story delivery through level design, environmental details, and the reverse-chronological loop structure. The player fights backwards through life experiences in a shattered snow globe (META-VERSE).

## Requirements

- Reverse temporal level progression (later memories first)
- Environmental storytelling (level geometry and props convey narrative)
- Loop reset mechanic that carries forward player knowledge
- 4 level sequence: Combat 1, Combat 2, Rest, Ending (per PROJECT_PLAN.md)

## Open Design Questions

- How much explicit narrative vs purely environmental
- Reset mechanic: what persists between loops (knowledge, items, stats)
- Memory distortion: how levels change across loops
- Ending conditions and variations
