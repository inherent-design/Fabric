# Sound Design

> **Not yet implemented.** Design placeholder for a confirmed game feature.

## Purpose

Audio content design: sound effect palette, music direction, ambient soundscapes, dynamic mixing for combat and exploration.

## Requirements

- Material interaction sounds (footsteps on stone vs sand, block break/place)
- Function casting audio (per-function signature sounds)
- NPC audio (idle chatter, aggro bark, transformation sounds)
- Ambient layers (wind, water, cave reverb)
- Music system (adaptive intensity based on combat state)

## Open Design Questions

- Music style and mood (dreamlike per AESTHETIC.md, or more dynamic)
- Number of ambient layers per biome
- Priority system for overlapping sound effects
- Reverb zones tied to world geometry or manual placement
